Focus OS Privacy Policy

Effective 12 August 2026

Focus OS is a personal task, project and meeting manager. This page explains what data the app handles, where it lives, and what we never do with it.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address and a securely hashed password, or your Google identity if you sign in with Google on the web.
  • Your content: the tasks, projects, notes and meetings you create, images you attach to tasks, and meeting recordings and transcripts you choose to make. Meeting recordings and their transcripts are stored in Google Cloud Storage; everything else lives in our Supabase database.
  • Calendar connection (optional): if you connect Google Calendar, we store the connection tokens and write events to a dedicated "Focus OS" calendar in your Google account. You can disconnect at any time.

Your tasks, projects and meetings are only accessible to your own account. Images attached to tasks are stored at hard-to-guess public links: they are not listed anywhere, but anyone who has the exact link can view that image (this is also what lets you hand an image link to an AI assistant from the app). Your session is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in.

Voice features

When you use Brain Dump, your microphone audio is streamed in real time to Google's Gemini API to be turned into text and tasks; the live audio is processed and not kept as a recording. Meeting recordings that you explicitly start are stored (in Google Cloud Storage) with the meeting and transcribed, so you can read them back. If you use the dictation feature in the AI handoff dialog, that recording is transcribed by OpenAI's Whisper service. You are always the one who starts and stops any capture.

Emails

We send transactional email only (through Resend): password resets, task and meeting shares you initiate, and their notifications. No marketing lists, no newsletters.

What we never do

  • No advertising and no advertising trackers.
  • No cross-site tracking or analytics profiles.
  • No selling or renting your data to anyone.

Services we rely on

Supabase (database, task-image storage, sign-in), Google (Gemini AI for voice and meeting processing, Cloud Storage for meeting recordings, and Calendar/sign-in if you use them), OpenAI (Whisper transcription for the AI handoff dictation), Resend (email delivery), and GitHub Pages (serving the app itself). Each processes only what the feature needs.

Your choices

You can delete your tasks, projects, meetings and recordings in the app at any time. To delete your entire account and its data, or to ask anything about this policy, contact igor.sesar@ais.ae and we will action it promptly.

We will update this page if the app's data handling changes; the effective date above always reflects the current version.
